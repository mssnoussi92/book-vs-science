## L'agent contre-preuve

⚖️ low holds; nothing reaches moderate. Taken: c2 rebalanced (Wegner, Lyubomirsky, Pavani against; Mauss 2012, McGuirk for), still mixed; culture is no switch (Takai); card line corrected. Not taken: c3 « mixed »: Sahdra's attachment items are worry items, Huang's cost is tiny and exploratory. c3 stays contradicted, low; the nuance becomes « it is how you chase it ».

- wegner-1993-mood : contradicts c2: trying to make one's mood better works with a free mind; only under mental load does mood move the opposite way. A condition, not a law; no replication on mood found
- lyubomirsky-2011-will : contradicts c2: students who wanted to become happier, and kept the effort, gained more from happiness activities (will not randomized; small effects; the authors design these activities)
- pavani-2025-goals : contradicts c1, c2 and c3: in 100 French women, wanting more happiness for tomorrow went with more intense happiness the next day, through savoring (diary, association; abstract read on HAL)
- mauss-2012-lonely : supports c2 in the book's sense (« a negative experience »): induced valuing of happiness raised loneliness; same lab as Mauss 2011, no replication
- mcguirk-2018-rumination : supports c2: stressing the importance of happiness raised rumination after a failure; independent lab, no replication
- takai-2023-japan : against a cultural switch: in Japan valuing happiness goes with more rumination, once more strongly among the most interdependent. Ford 2015 cannot become « in Asia it is the reverse »
- sahdra-2025-striving : offered against « c3 contradicted »; not taken: its « experiential attachment » items are worry items (« I worried about my positive emotions fading », full text). Kept as a nuance: doing one's best to stay happy helped one group, harmed the other only through worry
- huang-2024-valuing : the card: r = .24 was a random-intercept correlation from the preprint (dropped); the plain wish is followed by a little more negative affect as well as positive (exploratory); one item near its ceiling (mean 6.1 of 7)
- mauss-2011-seeking : fragile main support of c2: about 17 women per cell, effects at p = .02 to .05; its correction unread (APA 403), three concordant clues point to the title; Study 2 figures from the author manuscript
- 10.1016/j.tics.2026.03.001 : Mauss & Ford 2026, narrative review by the same lab: the consequences depend on how people pursue happiness (five aspects, not only worry): backs the nuance « it is how you chase it »
- 10.1080/17439760.2026.2651079 : Catalino, Tov & Hoorn 2026: five preregistered studies, prioritizing positivity goes with more well-being and more hypomania; not added (Hansenne 2021 already carries it)
- 10.1177/1948550611419677 : Conner & Reid 2011: rating one's happiness often does not lower it on average, but does among the more depressed: no takeaway that says « watch your happiness »
- 10.1371/journal.pone.0216588 : White, Uttl & Holder 2019: programs to become happier help a little once small-sample bias is corrected (r ≈ .10): indirect against c2
- 10.1007/s12144-021-02080-0 : Lambert et al. 2021: the only Arab trial found (120 Emirati students): a 6-week happiness program raised positive emotion, nothing else, no backfire; indirect
- 10.1111/aphw.12391 : Basurrah et al. 2022: positive psychology programs in 10 Arab countries appear effective, with risk of bias (systematic review): applicability, indirect
- 10.1007/s11482-025-10462-w : Lou et al. 2025, 43 countries: more religious cultures idealize happiness less; the premise « always wanting more » may fit an Egyptian audience less
- 10.3389/fpsyg.2022.949615 : Valdivia-Salas et al. 2022, 168 Spanish women, cross-sectional: valuing happiness goes with more negative affect; weak support for c1, not added
