- *Nour : meter 2, anxious (sur le mot 1 de l2 · jusqu'à son effet suivant)*

- **Nour** *(tired, honest, a little lost, not a complaint; the smile fades on « بس »)* : Yes… but not how I imagined.  `2.6 s`

## 3. Ce que dit le livre

*Carte du livre* : Wanting more good feelings… is itself a bad feeling.

- **Adasa** *(curious, plain, reporting what the book says; stress on « أقل »)* : The book says: the more you chase being happy… the less satisfied you are.  `5.8 s`
- **Adasa** *(warm, the book's own example, warm on « بيحس بالوحدة »)* : Like someone longing to be loved… who feels lonelier.  `3.9 s`

## 4. Le verdict : ⚖️ مش بالظبط

*Carte d'étude* : 8,331 Dutch · valuing happiness: better off · as it grew: joy and distress
*Nuance* (dite) : Those who worry about their happiness are worse off… those who seek it with others, more satisfied.

- **Adasa** *(skeptical, a small pause before the answer)* : And science? Not exactly.  `1.9 s`
- **Adasa** *(neutral, calm, factual; the big panel first, « في المتوسط » plain)* : 8,331 Dutch people: those who care about happiness are, on average, better off.  `5.5 s`
- **Adasa** *(neutral, « بس » turns it; the experiment told simply, a light stress on « أقل »)* : But in one experiment, people who read an article praising happiness… enjoyed a nice film less.  `6.7 s`

## 5. À retenir (suggestion à faible risque)

- **Adasa** *(warm, simple, a suggestion not an order; « زي نور » light, warm on « اللي بتحبهم »)* : Try it like Nour: instead of measuring your joy… sit with your loved ones. It may help.  `7.0 s`
- *Nour : point, happy (sur le mot 7 de l1)*
- *orientation* : If the distress lasts for weeks and affects your life, talk to a psychologist.
