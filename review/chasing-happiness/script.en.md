## 2. Scène s1 · beach · replay-twice · se termine a-bit-better

- **Adasa** *(neutral, setting the scene, light; a small smile on « رحلة الشركة »)* : This is Nour, on the company trip.  `3.0 s`
- **Nour** *(happy, to herself, excited but tense, a push on « لازم »)* : I have to enjoy every minute!  `2.6 s`
- **Nour** *(anxious, to herself, checking, a little let down on « المفروض أكتر »)* : Am I happy enough?… It should be more!  `3.5 s`
- *Nour : meter 2, anxious (sur le mot 1 de l2 · jusqu'à son effet suivant)*
- **Karim** *(happy, light, friendly, coming up to her on the sand)* : The sea's lovely, isn't it?  `2.3 s`
- **Nour** *(tired, honest, a little lost, not a complaint; the smile fades on « بس »)* : Lovely… but not what I imagined.  `2.6 s`
- *Nour : tired (1.8 s · seulement avec h1)*
  - à l'écran, sans voix : And your best day… was there a meter?
- *Nour : rewind (2.2 s)*
- **Adasa** *(warm, announcing the replay, light; a small stress on « من غير عدّاد »)* : Let's play it again… this time, no meter.  `3.5 s`
- *Nour : breathe, relieved (sur le mot 4 de n2)*
- **Karim** *(happy, light, friendly, same as before)* : The sea's lovely, isn't it?  `2.3 s`
- **Nour** *(happy, content, not thrilled; « حلو » without « بس »; points to the colleagues)* : Lovely… let's go sit with the others.  `3.0 s`

## 3. Ce que dit le livre

*Carte du livre* : Wanting more good feelings… is itself a bad feeling.

- **Adasa** *(curious, plain, reporting what the book says; stress on « أقل »)* : The book says: the harder you chase feeling good… the less content you are.  `5.8 s`
- **Adasa** *(warm, the book's reason made concrete; « أنا مبسوط؟ » as a little question to oneself)* : Because every « am I happy? »… reminds you of what you lack.  `4.7 s`

## 4. Le verdict : ⚖️ مش بالظبط

*Carte d'étude* : 8,331 Dutch · valuing happiness: better off · as it grew: joy and distress
*Nuance* (dite) : Those who worry about their happiness are worse off… those who seek it with others, more satisfied.

- **Adasa** *(skeptical, a small pause before the answer)* : And science? Not exactly.  `1.9 s`
- **Adasa** *(neutral, calm, factual; the same weight on « مرة » and « ومرة »)* : Sometimes they found happiness-chasers less happy… sometimes happier.  `3.5 s`

## 5. À retenir (suggestion à faible risque)

- **Adasa** *(warm, simple, a suggestion not an order; « زي نور » light, warm on « واقعد مع الناس »)* : Try it like Nour: drop the meter… and sit with people. It may help.  `5.8 s`
- *Nour : point, happy (sur le mot 6 de l1)*
- *orientation* : If the distress lasts for weeks and affects your life, talk to a psychologist.
