# Script « chasing-happiness » · en

**Happy enough?**

## 1. Les hooks (un seul est montré par vidéo)

**h1 · challenge** (Adasa, première image challenge) `5.8 s`
- à l'écran : Recall your best day… measuring it?
- dit : Try it: recall the best day of your life… were you measuring your joy?

**h2 · paradox** (Adasa, première image close-up) `7.5 s`
- à l'écran : Chase happiness… and it flies away?
- dit : They say happiness is like a butterfly: the more you chase it, the more it flies off… really?
- *Nour : meter 2, anxious*

**h3 · confession** (Nour, première image close-up) `7.5 s`
- à l'écran : Waiting all year… then: that's it?
- dit : I wait a whole year for the holiday… and on its first day I say: is that it?
- *Nour : shrug, meter 2, amused, regarde l'objectif*

**h4 · number** (Adasa, première image text-over-scene) `5.0 s`
- à l'écran : 8,331 people: value happiness, less happy?
- dit : 8,331 people: those who care more about happiness… less happy, or happier?
- *Nour : meter 2, neutral*

**h5 · question** (Karim, première image text-over-scene) `5.8 s`
- à l'écran : Asking yourself: happy enough?
- dit : And you? On a day out, do you ask yourself: am I happy enough?
- *Karim : point, amused, regarde l'objectif*
- *Nour : meter 2, anxious*

## 2. Scène s1 · beach · replay-twice · se termine a-bit-better

- **Adasa** *(neutral, setting the scene, light; a small smile on « للبحر »)* : This is Nour, on the work trip to the sea.  `4.3 s`
- **Nour** *(happy, to herself, excited but tense, a push on « لازم »)* : I have to enjoy every minute!  `2.6 s`
- **Nour** *(anxious, to herself, checking, a little let down on « المفروض أكتر »)* : Am I happy enough?… It should be more!  `3.5 s`
- *Nour : meter 2, anxious (sur le mot 1 de l2 · jusqu'à son effet suivant)*
- **Karim** *(happy, light, friendly, coming up to her on the sand)* : The sea's lovely, isn't it?  `2.3 s`
- **Nour** *(tired, honest, a little lost, not a complaint; the smile fades on « بس »)* : Lovely… but not what I imagined.  `2.6 s`
- *Nour : tired (1.2 s · seulement avec h1)*
  - à l'écran, sans voix : And on the best day of your life… was there a meter?
- *Nour : rewind (2.2 s)*
- **Adasa** *(warm, announcing the replay, light; a small stress on « من غير عدّاد »)* : Let's play it again… this time, no meter.  `3.5 s`
- *Nour : breathe, relieved (sur le mot 4 de n2)*
- **Karim** *(happy, light, friendly, same as before)* : The sea's lovely, isn't it?  `2.3 s`
- **Nour** *(happy, content, not thrilled; pointing to the others further along the beach)* : Yes! Come, let's sit with the others.  `3.0 s`

## 3. Ce que dit le livre

*Carte du livre* : Wanting more good feelings… is itself a bad feeling.

- **Adasa** *(curious, plain, reporting what the book says; stress on « أقل »)* : The book says: the harder you chase feeling good… the less content you are.  `5.8 s`
- **Adasa** *(warm, the book's reason made concrete; « أنا مبسوط؟ » as a little question to oneself)* : Because every « am I happy? »… reminds you of what you lack.  `4.7 s`

## 4. Le verdict : ⚖️ مش بالظبط

*Carte d'étude* : 8,331 Dutch · valuing happiness: generally better · valuing more: same satisfaction, mixed feelings
*Nuance* (dite) : Not the wish… worrying about your joy is linked to feeling worse, and seeking it with people to more satisfaction.

- **Adasa** *(skeptical, a small pause before the answer)* : And science? Not exactly.  `1.9 s`
- **Adasa** *(neutral, calm, factual; the same weight on « مرة » and « ومرة »)* : Sometimes they found happiness-chasers less happy… sometimes better off.  `3.9 s`

## 5. À retenir (suggestion à faible risque)

- **Adasa** *(warm, simple, a suggestion not an order; warm on « شاركها »)* : Try it: instead of measuring your joy… share it with someone. It may help.  `5.8 s`
- *Nour : point, happy (sur le mot 6 de l1)*
- *orientation* : If joy has been missing for weeks, talk to a psychologist.
