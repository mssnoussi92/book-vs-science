## L'agent contre-preuve

⚖️ holds, ✅ and ❌ excluded. Taken: c1 narrowed to the idealized dream (Cole, Hallford: nuances); c3 mixed (Kizilcec 2020, Kudrna, Kędra against; bias and allegiance in Wang); c4 rewritten on Woolley 2022; c2 very low, so the verdict is very low; the participant count left the card; the nuance said moved from c1 to c3. Not taken: splitting c3, a fifth claim.

- cole-2021-simulation : offered against c1 and c2: g = .23 for imagined success is a subgroup of an omnibus test that is not significant (p = .16), against no simulation, not against questioning thoughts: a nuance of both, not a contradiction
- hallford-2026-future : preregistered, 134 adults in Australia: thinking of the outcome raised reported motivation right after, the steps lowered perceived control; no effort or performance measured and the process arm did not take (dz = 0.07): a nuance of c1 and c2
- 10.14746/ssllt.2018.8.4.2 : Al-Hoorie 2018 (and Kim 2026, 10.3390/educsci16060892): the ideal L2 self goes with more intended effort; correlations, the measure confounded with self-confidence, i.e. the expectations c1 sets apart from fantasies: not taken
-  : no independent replication of positive fantasies (Kappes 2011, Oettingen 2002) in the works citing them: every retest comes from the same laboratory; c1 stays low and supports only its narrow reading
- marszal-wisniewska-2014-weight : for c2: the process group lost more weight at five weeks, study 1 only (about 10 women per group, weight measure not described); study 2 shows no process advantage: added, c2 stays mixed
- styk-2021-maze : for c2: more perseverance after process simulation, on easy mazes one leaves at will, alternating assignment, skewed data: added with a downgrade for risk of bias
- 10.1038/s41598-021-02036-1 : Prokopowicz & Byrka 2021: process against outcome not tested as such in the abstract, overall p = .073: not taken
- alghazo-2020-simulation : the largest randomized head-to-head (269 Saudi women students): no difference at the midterm or in engagement; outcome ahead early in the term, process later: contradicts c2, which becomes very low after the second review
- wang-2021-mcii : the card: 87% of its 15,907 participants are in one online study whose effect held only in English-speaking countries; 13 of 21 articles by MCII's creators; Egger t = 5.46, 0.242 after trim-and-fill: no participant count on the card
- kizilcec-2020-scale : preregistered, 12,879 students of individualist countries: MCII did not raise course completion, its interval excludes the original gains: c3 mixed, and the nuance said
- kizilcec-2017-culture : no effect in collectivist or « balanced » countries, an effect only in the English-speaking cluster; MC and II alone changed nothing
- kudrna-2025-workplace : independent preregistered cluster trial, 225 employees whose control states a wish: no added progress at four weeks; imprecise (28 of 60 workplaces, 29% lost)
- kedra-2026-online : two small online trials in Poland, both null, powered for a medium effect only
- 10.1080/00224545.2022.2163610 : Riddell 2023: MCII did not support goal attainment for 111 cyclists; same direction as Kudrna and Kędra, not added
- 10.1111/dar.13553 : Cooke 2022: if-then plans on alcohol, null at university and online; plans alone, not the MCII package: not added
- wittleder-2025-sham : for c3, the exact comparator: WOOP against a sham WOOP that dreams twice, fewer heavy drinking days at 1 and 2 months; a feasibility pilot (86 randomized, unequal loss), creators co-authors
- armitage-2017-kuwait : if-then plans alone in Kuwait (216 adults, questionnaires in Arabic, nationality not reported): more weight lost at 6 months, d = .38; no mental contrasting: a nuance of c3, refused for the screen
