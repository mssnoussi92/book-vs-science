## L'agent contre-preuve

⚖️ holds, low; ❌ is not defensible. Taken: c1 tested on Manson's words ($75,000 or $120,000): mixed; c2 mixed (Akkiraju, Jebb vs Killingsworth, Arslan), its nuance replaced by « money weighs more on judging one's life than on the day's mood »; c3 mixed, low; c4 on Thomson, Dwyer, Diener, not Howell; Thomson on screen.

- thomson-2022-income : meta-analysis, 86 studies: leaving poverty d = 0.38 (wellbeing) and 0.13 (mental health) vs 0.16 and 0.01 for other rises; GRADE low or very low: c3 mixed, c4 supported; on screen
- arslan-2024-flattening : independent reanalysis of the same data: no clear flattening before $200,000; the « least happy » plateau is weak: that nuance is withdrawn
- rohrer-2024-quantile : a percentile is not a group of people: nothing on screen may say « for the least happy people »; no covariates, so no causal reading
- 10.1073/pnas.2322160121 : Killingsworth, Kahneman & Mellers 2024 reply (refused by the verifier as a letter): K&D's $75,000 was the midpoint of a $60-90K band; threshold $100,000 after inflation; all quantiles but the 15th rise
- akkiraju-2025-stress : 2.05 million US adults: above ~$63,000, stress more likely: supports c2, so c2 cannot be « contradicts » under the grid
- bennedsen-2024-plateau : emotional plateau near $200,000, hedged by its author: a plateau may exist, far above $75,000-120,000
- kuhn-2008-postcode : random postcode lottery (about 8 months of income): not happier six months later: supports c3
- oh-2025-trajectories : three panels (US, South Korea): changes in income unrelated to changes in well-being: supports c3
- wollburg-2023-cash : 17 RCTs, poor households: depression lower while cash is paid, not sustained 2-9 years after: supports « not lastingly », indirect
- raschke-2018-lottery : German winners: no mental-health gain (high education), a loss (low education), yet they report being happier; at odds with Gardner 2006
- diener-2010-wealth : income predicts life evaluation, much less feelings; basic needs mediate only partly: the nuance said, and against the « needs » explanation
- nilsson-2024-cantril : the ladder question primes wealth: a risk of bias for the life-evaluation evidence of c1
- eid-2024-egypt : Egypt: average happiness 4.8/10; Egyptians judge their lives poorly but feel fairly well day to day: the nuance holds for Egypt
- 10.21203/rs.3.rs-5355955/v1 : Tamberg et al. 2024, preprint (Research Square), no published version: once needs are met, income adds marginally to life evaluation; refused by the verifier; it controls for the needs income buys
- 10.1037/bul0000258 : Tan et al. 2020, meta-analysis of 357 studies: the income link is stronger in wealthier samples, against Howell 2008; refused by the verifier (false positive on « the objective »)
- 10.1371/journal.pone.0079358 : Proto & Rustichini 2013: country-level satisfaction flattens above ~$15,000 GDP per head, peaks near $30,000: aggregate, indirect for c1

- 10.1038/s41598-026-59695-1 : Fors Connolly et al. 2026: across 33 European countries GDP goes with both emotions and satisfaction: against Röck 2025
