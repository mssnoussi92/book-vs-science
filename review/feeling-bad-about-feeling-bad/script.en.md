**Upset about being upset?**

## 1. Les hooks (un seul est montré par vidéo)

**h1 · challenge** (Adasa, première image challenge) `5.8 s`
- à l'écran : Don't think of a white bear
- dit : Try it: don't think of a white bear… until the end of this video.

**h2 · paradox** (Adasa, première image close-up) `5.0 s`
- à l'écran : « Stop being upset »… brings it back?
- dit : You tell yourself « stop being upset »? You may be bringing it back.
- *Karim : fist-clench, spiral 2, annoyed*

**h3 · confession** (Karim, première image close-up) `5.0 s`
- à l'écran : Upset… then upset about it
- dit : I get upset… and then I get upset that I got upset.
- *Karim : cloud, ashamed, regarde l'objectif*
- *Karim : spiral 2*

**h4 · number** (Adasa, première image challenge) `4.7 s`
- à l'écran : 31 studies… what happened?

- *Karim : thought-bubble white-bear, anxious*

**h5 · question** (Nour, première image text-over-scene) `4.3 s`

## 2. Scène s1 · office · replay-twice · se termine a-bit-better

- **Adasa** *(neutral, setting the scene, calm, a little suspense on « والرد لسه واصل »)* : This is Karim. He sent a proposal at work… and the answer just came in.  `6.3 s`
- *Karim : phone rejected, cloud, sad (0.8 s · jusqu'à la fin de la partie)*
- **Karim** *(sad, to himself, deflated, reading the message on his phone)* : The manager turned down the proposal…  `2.6 s`
- **Karim** *(annoyed, to himself, scolding himself under his breath)* : You're upset over something this small?  `2.6 s`
- *Karim : spiral 2, anxious (0.8 s · jusqu'à son effet suivant)*
- **Karim** *(angry, to himself, through his teeth, forcing it away)* : Stop being upset already!  `1.9 s`
- *Karim : head-in-hands, spiral 3, ashamed (1 s · jusqu'à son effet suivant)*
- **Nour** *(curious, light, friendly, passing by his desk)* : Karim? What's wrong?  `1.4 s`
- **Karim** *(ashamed, too fast, a forced smile, hiding it)* : Nothing… I'm fine.  `1.4 s`
- *Karim : phone rejected, rewind (2.2 s)*
- **Adasa** *(warm, announcing the replay, light, a small stress on « مش هيلوم نفسه »)* : Let's play it again… but this time Karim won't blame himself.  `4.7 s`
- **Karim** *(sad, to himself, slow, honest, no drama; a small breath before « تعبت فيه »)* : Yeah, I'm upset… and that's normal. I worked hard on it.  `4.7 s`
- *Karim : breathe, sad (1 s)*
- **Nour** *(curious, light, friendly, same as before)* : Karim? What's wrong?  `1.4 s`
- **Karim** *(sad, a little sad but steady, open with her, already planning)* : A bit upset… the manager turned down the proposal. I'll fix it and send it again.  `6.7 s`

## 3. Ce que dit le livre

*Carte du livre* : Feeling bad isn't the problem. Blaming yourself for it makes it grow.

- **Adasa** *(curious, plain, reporting what the book says; a beat before « الحل؟ »)* : The book says: blame yourself for being upset and it grows; push it away and it comes back. The answer? Accept it, no blame.  `9.8 s`

## 4. Le verdict : ⚖️ مش بالظبط

*Carte d'étude* : Analysis of 31 studies · lab experiments · about thoughts, not feelings
*Nuance* (écrite seulement, carte tenue 2.6 s) : Accept the feeling… not the situation

- **Adasa** *(skeptical, a small pause before the answer)* : And science? Not exactly.  `1.9 s`
- **Adasa** *(neutral, calm, factual; a beat before « بس ده ارتباط »)* : People who blame themselves for being upset tend to feel worse. But it's a link… and it's unclear which comes first.  `8.7 s`
- *Adasa : two-way (sur le mot 11 de l2)*
- **Adasa** *(neutral, calm, a hint of a smile on the last words)* : And « stop being upset »? It probably doesn't lower the feeling, and the thought you push away tends to come back more, later.  `9.1 s`
- *Karim : thought-bubble white-bear (sur le mot 11 de l3 · seulement avec h1, h4)*
- *Karim : thought-bubble phone-rejected (sur le mot 11 de l3 · seulement avec h2, h3, h5)*

## 5. À retenir (suggestion à faible risque)

- **Adasa** *(warm, simple, a suggestion not an order; the quote in Karim's calm tone)* : What Karim did, try it today when you're upset: « Yeah, I'm upset, and that's normal. » It may help.  `7.5 s`
- *Karim : breathe, neutral (0.6 s)*
- *orientation* : If this feeling lasts for weeks and affects your life, talk to a psychologist.
