## L'agent contre-preuve

The ⚖️ badge holds: nothing of equal or higher rank contradicts c1 or c2. Three corrections taken: c1 is low (no manipulation of self-judgment, one lab's line of work, a possible measurement bias), c2 is narrowed (it comes back afterwards; distraction excluded), c3 is mixed and low (Roos 2026, Dunn 2009). The takeaway stays a suggestion, and « معظم الدراسات من أمريكا وأوروبا » is said.

- mamat-2023-suppression-training : contradicts c2: three days of training to suppress thoughts lowered anxiety; one lab, no independent replication
- 10.1080/09658211.2023.2208791 : Wiechert et al. 2023: the preregistered replication of the paradigm Mamat & Anderson rely on failed (d = 0.03)
- dunn-2009-suppression-acceptance : contradicts c2 and c3: suppressing lowered the fear felt; accepting did not help
- roos-2026-visitor-mindset : contradicts c3: a session targeting acceptance did not lower distress (d = 0.13): the takeaway stays a suggestion
- gower-2026-act : nuances c3: ACT does no better than other psychotherapies (g = -0.01)
- hofmann-2009-anxiety : nuances c3: accepting did not calm felt anxiety more than suppressing; reappraisal did better
- wang-2020-ironic : nuances c2: the rebound after is confirmed, but during the effort the thought grows only under mental load
- nickerson-2025-refugees : nuances c1: the only sample found that includes Arabic speakers; a two-way link with PTSD only
- 10.1177/1073191112438743 : Van Dam et al. 2012: Ford 2018 measures acceptance with self-critical items (possible measurement bias), partly answered by Willroth 2023
- 10.2466/03.20.pr0.114k22w4 : Hu et al. 2014 (meta-analysis, 48 studies): hiding emotions costs less in East Asia (r = .06 vs .19); Qiu 2026, Soto 2011, Butler 2007, Schunk 2021 agree
- 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1738432 : Alfayez 2026 (Saudi Arabia, cross-sectional): suppression goes with more distress, the Western pattern; no Egyptian study found
