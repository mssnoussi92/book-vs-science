## L'agent contre-preuve

⚖️ low holds: no claim reaches a net moderate direction. Taken: c1/c2 rewritten with « beyond a certain point » and read as « near zero on average, present under conditions »; the jam study is contested and no longer evidence; c4 now contradicts (63-country meta-analysis, the only Arab sample); Gu 2013 added for c3, Li 2022 weighed less; card line widened, with its condition.

- 10.1016/j.jcps.2015.07.001 : Chernev corrigendum, not linked by Crossref: the mean-effect section holds b = .17 and .41 (p < .001) but also b = .04 (p > .4). Chernev is now read as an effect under conditions, not as a meta-analysis that contradicts Scheibehenne on the average
- mcshane-2018-multilevel : reanalysis (Böckenholt co-author of Chernev): the effect varies by outcome measure, so c1 (satisfaction) and c2 (deferral) cannot rest on one pooled figure
- scheibehenne-2010-overload : the card line pooled « satisfaction » alone: the mean covers satisfaction or the wish to choose; a mean near zero alone reads as ❌ under a ⚖️, so Chernev's condition is written under it
- reutskaja-2009-satiate : inverted U, with reutskaja-2018-neural: c1 and c2 rewritten with Manson's own « beyond a certain point »; the monotone version was stronger than the book
- yeung-2025-selfcare : randomized, Hong Kong, 652 adults: 4 or 16 self-care exercises beat 1 on practice and decision satisfaction; no loss up to 16
- lindkvist-2022-donation : preregistered: donating did not change from 2 to 80 charities, whatever preference certainty: against c2 and against Chernev's preference-uncertainty moderator
- reutskaja-2022-deprivation : six nations, 7,436 people: too little choice is the norm outside the US and weighs more on satisfaction than too much; by Iyengar and Schwartz themselves
- fischer-2011-autonomy : meta-analysis, 63 countries, 420,599 people: more individualism goes with more well-being: c4 moves from mixed to contradicts (association, autonomy rather than number of options)
- zayed-2026-autonomy : the only Arab sample found (328 Arabic-speaking adults): personal autonomy goes with less distress (cross-sectional); nothing Arab on the number of options, deferral or reversibility
- fasolo-2024-freedom : field + preregistered (669): telling people they may choose as much as they wish raises satisfaction with what they chose: nuances c3 and c4
- gu-2013-closure : support for c3 the draft had missed: acts of closure raise satisfaction with choices from large sets (4 studies, consumer choices, no replication)
- bertrand-2010-advertising : support for c2 in a real decision (South Africa, randomized mailers): fewer example loans, more take-up; an advertisement, not a choice set
- iyengar-2000-demotivating : the jam study enters content/contested.json (average near zero, failed tests, a preregistered null; no multi-lab replication) and is no longer counted as evidence: never shown as a fact
- li-2022-reversibility : risk of bias found in the full text (manipulation check d reported as 0.98 for means implying about 10; dummy coding written both ways): kept, weighed less
- 10.1177/09567976251400333 : Hu, Moon & VanEpps 2026, six preregistered experiments (10,092 US adults) whose premise is that larger sets yield better-liked choices; full text not read: not used
- 10.1017/s1930297500003247 : Roets, Schwartz & Guan 2012: maximizers report less well-being only in choice-rich societies (US, Western Europe), not in China; the one limited support for c4, Schwartz co-author; not added
- 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.42949 : Altinger 2025, 402 US physicians, randomized: more alternatives meant less status quo, not more deferral (1 vs 2-4 options); not added
- 10.1037/0022-3514.84.1.97 : Chirkov 2003 (incl. Turkey) and Welzel & Inglehart 2010 (about 80 societies): autonomy or agency goes with well-being; same direction as c4's contradiction, not added
